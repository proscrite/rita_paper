\begin{abstract}
% Short abstract placeholder. Replace with final text.
We present measurements of electron recombination in high-pressure xenon using the RITA radium-ion tagging setup. Using alpha-tagged recoil events and X-ray calibration, we quantify the detected-electron yield and recombination fraction for Ra224 and Rn220 recoils under different drift and EL field settings.
\end{abstract}
