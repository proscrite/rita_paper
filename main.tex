\begin{abstract}
    % Short abstract placeholder. Replace with final text.
    WWe present measurements of electron recombination in high-pressure xenon using the RITA
    radium-ion tagging setup. Using alpha-tagged recoil events and X-ray calibration, we quantify
    the detected-electron yield and recombination fraction for \isotope{Ra}{224} and
    \isotope{Rn}{220} recoils under different drift and EL field settings.
\end{abstract}
